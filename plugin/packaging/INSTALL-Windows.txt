eureka! by Tootable Instruments - Windows (64-bit)
Built on the Mutable Instruments Ambika. GPL-3.0.

VST3 PLUG-IN
1. Copy the "eureka!.vst3" folder to:
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. eureka! is listed under Tootable Instruments.
   (Ableton Live: Options > Preferences > Plug-Ins, turn on "Use VST3
   Plug-In System Folders", then click Rescan.)

STANDALONE APP
Run "eureka!.exe" from any folder. Choose your audio and MIDI devices in
Options > Audio/MIDI Settings.

This build is not code-signed. If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows
protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

WINDOW SIZE
Drag the bottom-right corner, or right-click an empty part of the window
background to choose a size (60% to 200%). The size is saved with your
project.

SEPARATE OUTPUTS (VST3)
Each voice can play on its own stereo output ("Voice 1" to "Voice 6"): set
its Output to "own output" on the Multi page and enable that output in your
DAW.

Presets are saved in Documents\eureka\presets.

User guide: https://github.com/Milkman8008/eureka-vst
